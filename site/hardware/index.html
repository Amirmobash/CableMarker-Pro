Important electrical checks

Laser UART

The exact legacy sensor model is undocumented. Verify its voltage levels and physical interface before connecting it to 3.3 V ESP32 pins.

Servo supply

Use a supply with enough transient current. A servo powered from an undersized board regulator can reset the ESP32.

Trigger input

GPIO 19 is 3.3 V logic. Industrial 12/24 V trigger signals need isolation or level conversion.
