Long-run workflow

Cable and pipe marking over runs up to about 100 m

The firmware is numerically ready for long runs. Whether 100 m works in practice is determined by the laser rangefinder and the measurement environment.

What actually matters at long distance

Sensor rating

Use a rangefinder whose datasheet explicitly covers the required distance and target conditions.

Target quality

Reflectivity, incidence angle, ambient light and dust can reduce reliable range.

Mechanical reference

The optical origin and physical marking point must be calibrated together.

Recommended validation

  1. Verify UART electrical compatibility.
  2. Test repeatability at short, medium and maximum distance.
  3. Calibrate the mechanical offset.
  4. Dry-run the marker before enabling physical contact.
  5. Record the sensor model and calibration for the hardware revision.
Software range: 100 m = 100,000 mm, which is comfortably inside the firmware's integer representation.
